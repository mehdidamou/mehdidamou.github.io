01 / About

Every hero needs
a sidekick

I learned a job, then a profession, and finally how to be.

I'm an engineer by training and a builder at heart. Before consulting, I co-founded a streetwear brand and a PR & events company: designing products, running production, raising money, throwing parties.

In 2009 I joined McKinsey and found far more than a firm. I found communities: offices from Switzerland, Benelux and France to Montréal, the Africa office that became my second family, and the transformation community (RTS, now MT) that became a family of its own. The Algerian community will always hold a special place in my heart.

Transformations are never a solo act. The real heroes are the leaders and teams who take their institutions to territories they've never been. My role was to stand beside them, bringing the tools, the method and the mindset to change performance and culture at the same time, and to make that change last.

Together with those teams, that has meant 20+ companies and institutions transformed, $30bn+ in returns, and 40+ executives who grew new super powers along the way. Their wins, my privilege.

LESSON 01

Impact is won by the heart, not by ideas.

I joined convinced the goal was to be the smartest person in the room. Serving clients taught me otherwise. It was an unexpected, sometimes painful lesson, and one of the most valuable I carry. I was humbly part of deep transformations in which women and men grew toward nobler aspirations.

LESSON 02

Caring and meritocratic. Both. Always.

You can build an environment that creates real opportunities for remarkable people while keeping the bar uncompromisingly high. I didn't learn this in a straight line. To everyone I learned it with: thank you.
